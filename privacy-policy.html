Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Snow Globe (package name com.phonensoft.snowglobe, "the app") handles information. The app is developed and provided by J.Biglion ("we", "us").

  • The app has no sign-up or login, and we do not run a server.
  • We do not collect, store or sell your personal information.
  • Your globes and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device information such as the advertising ID.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are requested, and you can change it any time in Settings.

1. Information stored on your device

The following information is created while you use the app and is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us.

  • Globes you decorate (background, items, positions, names)
  • App settings (snow, sound, timer, display mode, language and so on)
  • Items you have unlocked by watching a rewarded ad
  • Your ad consent choice (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you have turned on Android backup (for example, Google backup), this data may be included in your device backup under Google's policies. You can delete the data at any time by deleting a globe in the app, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app.

2. Photos and videos

When you tap "Save photo" or "Save video", the image or video of your globe is saved to your device's gallery. When you tap "Share", it is sent only to the app you choose. We cannot access these files.

3. Permissions

PermissionWhy it is used
NotificationsTo tell you when the focus, rest or sleep timer ends. You can turn this off at any time.
Foreground service (media playback)To keep the timer and background music running when you leave the app.
VibrationTo vibrate when the timer ends.
Storage (Android 9 and below only)To save photos and videos to the gallery. On Android 10 and above, no permission is needed.
Motion sensor (accelerometer)To make a snowstorm when you shake your phone and to let the snow drift when you tilt it on the live wallpaper. Sensor values are used only on your device and are not stored or sent anywhere.
InternetTo load ads (added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK).

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: native ads (collection and exit screens), banner ads (help screens), interstitial ads (when saving a photo or video) and rewarded ads (to unlock some items, only when you choose to watch).

To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • The advertising ID of your device and other device identifiers
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • IP address and the approximate location it indicates
  • Ad interactions (impressions and taps) and diagnostic information

This information is used by Google to deliver ads, measure performance, prevent fraud and, depending on your settings, show personalized ads. For details, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob and data privacy

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent the first time you open it, using Google's User Messaging Platform (a certified consent management platform under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework). Ads are requested only after this step. If you do not consent, you may still see ads, but they will be non-personalized or limited ads.

You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings › About › Privacy choices. Your consent choice is stored on your device.

Your choices: You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).

Legal basis (EEA/UK)

Personalized advertising and storing or accessing information on your device are based on your consent. Showing non-personalized ads, measuring ads and preventing fraud may be based on the legitimate interests of Google and its partners, as described in the consent form. You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to processing and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because we do not hold any of your personal data ourselves, requests about ad data are handled by Google; you can contact us and we will help where we can.

5. Information we collect

We (the developer) do not collect any personal information through the app. The app does not include analytics or crash-reporting tools, and it does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location or account details.

If you contact us by email, we will use your email address and message only to reply to you, and delete them once they are no longer needed.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties. Information processed by Google for advertising is handled under Google's own policies described above.

7. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for all ages (rated G). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by your device's security. Communication with Google's ad servers is encrypted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
