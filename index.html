Small joys, all in one globe

Leave it on and watch, or make it truly yours.

Shake for a snowstorm

Shake your phone or tap the globe and the snow swirls up. Leave it a while and the snow slowly piles up.

Decorate your own

Choose a sky, then place cabins, churches, fir trees, snowmen and gifts. Drag, resize and flip them however you like.

Focus & rest timer

Focus for 25 minutes, rest for 5, or drift off with a 30-minute sleep timer whose sound fades out gently.

Live wallpaper

Keep your globe on your home and lock screen. Tilt your phone and the snow drifts with it.

Save & share

Save the moment as a photo or a 5, 10 or 15-second video with music, and share it with friends.

Cozy music

Soft piano, a music box carol, a crackling fireplace or the winter wind. Night mode dims the globe after 7 PM.
